The plain-language privacy promise

Your footage
stays yours.

OhIc was built around a simple idea: understanding a private video should not require giving that video away.

01

No account to create.

Open the app and begin. There is no profile connecting your videos to a company database.

02

No cloud video library.

Your imports, captures, results, transcripts, identities, and conversations live on your Mac.

03

No training on your moments.

Your footage is not sent away to improve someone else’s model or advertising system.

04

No mystery about the code.

OhIc is open source. Anyone can inspect how it handles files, models, searches, and local history.

When OhIc does connect

Only for something
you asked for.

Local-first does not mean pretending the internet never exists. These are the moments where OhIc reaches out.

APP DOWNLOAD

GitHub serves the OhIc disk image when you choose to download it.

MODEL DOWNLOAD

Public model sources provide restoration and optional Pro models the first time you request them.

YOUTUBE IMPORT

YouTube is contacted only when you paste a permitted link and ask OhIc to inspect or download it.

PHONE CAMERA

Your paired phone connects directly to the Mac across the local network.
