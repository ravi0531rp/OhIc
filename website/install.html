Good to know

A few honest
answers.

Does OhIc upload my videos?

No. Video processing and AI inference happen locally. A permitted YouTube import naturally connects to YouTube when you ask for it, and model downloads connect to their public sources.

Will every model download when I open the app?

No. Downsize needs no model. The restoration model arrives on first enhancement. The larger Pro collection downloads only after you explicitly choose Download Pro.

Can I remove the models later?

Yes. The app keeps its managed files visible, and the optional Pro setup is stored separately from the application itself.

Does it work on an Intel Mac?

The current preview is built for Apple silicon. A separate Intel or universal build is not available yet.
